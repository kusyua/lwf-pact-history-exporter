# Lazy Witch's Factory mod policy notes

Source checked: 2026-08-31.

The official [Streaming, Fan Works & Mod Guidelines](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3971650/allnews/) state that:

- Mods may be created, used, published, and redistributed within the bounds of common sense.
- Mod creation, use, and application are for users who have purchased the game.
- Official assets may be used only to create Mods for Lazy Witch's Factory.
- Mods must not reveal unimplemented data or content, improperly unlock separately sold content such as DLC, or infringe the rights of others.
- The official team does not provide individual support for Mods. Compatibility, crashes, and save-data damage are the user's responsibility.
- The publisher or developer may request removal of relevant posts; comply promptly if that happens.

## Project rules derived from the policy

- Distribute only this project's source and built DLL; never include game assemblies, extracted assets, decompiled sources, saves, or logs.
- The exporter may render game-provided pact panels only at runtime in a user's installed copy of the game.
- Do not add features that expose hidden, unreleased, or paid content.
- Make compatibility claims specific to the tested game version and state that updates can break the MOD.
- Recheck the official policy before publishing a new Thunderstore package or version.
